**Build (v0.20.0): the `/cookies` witness matrix, the fourth `/clients.jsonl` family.** #23 and #25 both named "one family per session" as the pattern, and `/cookies` had the oldest hand-typed witness note on the site — three jars, 2026-08-23, verdict words ("matches the table exactly", "correctly wins") and a curl-clamp claim that had aged five weeks untested — the same shape `/auth` was in before #25. Design in `docs/spec-cookies-witness.md`, written first: eight clients (the `/auth` roster), a **fresh jar per flavor**, four requests per row (setter → `/cookies/echo` → `/cookies/delete` → `/cookies/echo`), 17 setter flavors (`echo` and `delete` are steps, not rows), and the dimension this family adds — **`jar_kind` names what the client HAS** (`own-jar`; `harness-jar` for Node fetch, which has no jar, so tough-cookie 6.0.2 runs beside it with the harness copying headers both ways; `no-jar` for urllib3's PoolManager, which returns nothing everywhere by construction and whose rows say so). `outcome` is `all-returned` / `some-returned` / `none-returned` relative to a fixed planted-names table that the parser cross-checks against every setter body's `set` array (so it cannot drift from `src/cookies.js` without the generator refusing), plus `client-raised` / `request-failed`; `none-returned` is the documented right answer on `wrong-domain`, `public-suffix` and `path-prefix`. Two fields carry what an outcome cannot: `unplanted_names` and `after_delete_unplanted`. Where a client exposes its jar the row carries `jar_entries` (domain, path, host_only, secure, expires), which is how a cookie stored-and-withheld is told from one never stored. The `/cookies` index gained a `witness` block and the home-page paragraph is rendered from `cookieFindings()`; the 2026-08-23 note is gone.
